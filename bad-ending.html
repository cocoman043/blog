Philippines: Bad Ending Extended

2022 May 26

The Covid-19 pandemic was 100 years ago. Global warming was solved with the help of quantum computers, carbon capture technology, and clean energy generation, 80 years ago. And here’s Maharlika, formerly known as the Philippine Islands, now an archipelagic province of China.

The citizens of Maharlika live great, happy, and satisfying lives. Rich in culture, tradition, and natural resources, the province continues to be the number one exporter of AI services. All citizens have access to free and high-quality healthcare and the youth studies in the top schools in the world, on the mainland. The public is content with how the government supports them in their daily lives and the government continues to handle everything.

It all started 90 years ago, during the Federal Administration of Prime Minister Sara Duterte. Being the most affected country on Earth by global warming in the 30s, the Republic of China gave its support by helping the country with rebuilding infrastructure destroyed by constant supertyphoons. From financial support, to partnerships, to becoming a part of China, all within 10 years' time.

There are still some kinks in the system such as the lack of opposition in the government, the pollution caused by the mass production of graphene, and the underground terrorist groups fighting for freedom on the outskirts of the province. But even with these, the people of Maharlika live in peace and prosperity. Mabuhay ang Maharlika.
